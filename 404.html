InfoDot is the front door.

InfoDot exists to help people start, operate, manage, and grow successful businesses by combining collective human experience, practical knowledge, AI-assisted guidance, and business intelligence from across the Dot.Eco-system.

Platforms in the Dot.Eco-system hand each other signed data, so a business sees one picture instead of five.
