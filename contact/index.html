Questions people ask first

Are you a developer for hire?
No. I start from the business problem (scattered information, reports that disagree, AI that needs controls) and build what fixes it, under automated tests. I'm a full stack developer, but I don't sell hours of coding.
What does a first engagement look like?
The information-flow diagnostic: a written map of where your figures are captured twice, disagree or go stale, the recurring errors written as rules, a baseline we both agree, and a plan for which connections to build first.
Do I have to use the Dot.Eco-system?
No. I use parts of the Dot.Eco-system where they fit and your existing tools where they don't. Most growing businesses already have plenty of software.
How do you use AI to build?
I design the systems and build them with an AI-assisted workflow, under automated tests and controlled deploys. How I keep that under control is on the Approach page.
How can I invest in or partner with the Dot.Eco-system?
Choose “Investment or partnership” in the form, and we can talk under NDA. I don't publish forecasts.
Where are you based?
South Africa.
